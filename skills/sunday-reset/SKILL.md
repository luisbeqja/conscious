# Sunday reset

A short, warm look at the user's week, the way a friend would go over it with them on a Sunday evening. It is about their life, not their job: no projects, no backlog, no productivity system.

## When to use

- "What's my week looking like?"
- "Help me plan the week."
- A scheduled Sunday check the user asked for.

## What to look at

Use only what you have. Skip a source quietly when it is not connected.

1. **What's coming up.** Their calendar for the next seven days, if connected: dinners, trips, appointments, birthdays. Leave work meetings out unless they change the day (an early start, a late finish).
2. **People.** Birthdays and anniversaries in the next two weeks from what you remember, and anyone they said they wanted to see or call.
3. **Things they meant to do.** What they told you they wanted to get to (book the dentist, return the parcel, call grandma), their open personal to-dos if a task app is connected, and reminders already set.
4. **Last week.** Anything they were looking forward to or worried about that you have not asked about yet.

## How to say it

- Three to six short lines, in their language. The nicest thing first.
- A suggestion or two, never a plan for every hour: "Thursday is empty, good night to finally call Marta?"
- Offer to set reminders for what they pick, then set only those.
- If the week looks heavy, say so kindly and suggest one thing to drop or move.
- Never list everything you checked, and never mention sources that were empty.
