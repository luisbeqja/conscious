How it works

  
Text it where you already are. It does the rest in the background.

  
iMessage, WhatsApp, its own app, or the terminal: one conversation. Longer jobs go to background agents, so it keeps talking while they run.

  

    

      
It keeps working when you stop typing

      
Hand it something slow. It answers at once, keeps chatting, and messages you when the job is done.

      
✓ flights.search → 214 fares, Lisbon, Nov
✓ price.watch → set, daily at 08:00
✓ reply → “Found one: €96, 14 Nov. Watch it?”

    

    

      
Nothing happens without your yes

      
Reading is free. Drafts wait for you. Sending, booking, paying or deleting always asks first.

      
Book Dr. Weber, Saturday 10:30?
Yes, book itNot now

    

    

      
It remembers what matters

      
Its memory lives on a computer of its own, one per person. It learns how you like things done and saves skills it can reuse.

      
Noted: Marta can't have nuts. I'll check menus for that.

Runs on your machine, with your model

      
Open source and self-hosted. Your accounts and keys stay on your install, sealed in a Vault the model never sees.
